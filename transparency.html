Transparency Seal

In compliance with Section 93 of the General Appropriations Act and National Budget Circular No. 542, the Camiling Water District (CWD) publishes the following transparency documents on this website.

The Transparency Seal is a certificate that CWD has complied with the requirements of Section 93 of the GAA, making government information accessible to every Filipino citizen it serves.

Basis: RA 11032 (Ease of Doing Business Act) · EO 2 s. 2016 (FOI) · NBC 542 · Section 93, GAA
