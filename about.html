About Camiling Water District

CAMIWD is a government-owned and controlled corporation (GOCC) chartered under Presidential Decree No. 198, established on December 6, 1976, and formally became a full-fledged water district on July 27, 1977 (LWUA [Board Resolution No. — confirm with Maam Bel]).

Mission

Camiling Water District exists to provide safe, sufficient, and affordable potable water service to the households, businesses, and institutions of Camiling, Tarlac — delivered with transparency, accountability, and responsiveness to the communities we serve.

Vision

To be a model government water utility in Central Luzon, recognized for dependable service, sound governance, and a modern, responsive relationship with every barangay under our care.

Leadership

Public-Private Partnership

CAMIWD operates in partnership with the private sector to improve service delivery while retaining full public ownership of the water system.

Joint Venture with PrimeWater Infrastructure Corporation

Camiling Water District (CAMIWD) operates under a Joint Venture Agreement (JVA) with PrimeWater Infrastructure Corporation for the financing, development, rehabilitation, upgrading, expansion, operation, and maintenance of the district's potable water supply and septage management systems.

This arrangement falls under the Philippine Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Program. Because water districts cannot, by law, be sold to private corporations, joint venture agreements such as this allow CAMIWD to retain public ownership of the water system while drawing on private-sector capital and technical expertise to improve service delivery to Camiling residents.

Note: PrimeWater Infrastructure Corporation has begun rebranding as Hiraya Water Corporation, following SEC approval in 2025. Both names may appear in official and historical documentation during the transition.
