Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from residents.

How do I apply for a new water connection?

Visit the Services page for the full requirements checklist, or come to the CWD office with a valid ID and proof of property/lot right to start an application.

Where can I pay my water bill?

GCash, Maya, Link.BizPortal, Coins.ph, AllBank, Bayad Center, 7-Eleven, Palawan Pawnshop, AllEasy, the PrimeWater app, or walk-in at the office. See the Payments page for details on each.

How do I report a leak?

Contact the office directly through the Contact page with the location of the leak. Mainline leaks are prioritized for repair.

My meter reading looks wrong. What do I do?

You can request a meter accuracy test through the Services page or by visiting the office directly.
