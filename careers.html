How to apply

1. Check requirements

Each posting lists its own qualification standards (education, experience, training, eligibility).

2. Submit application

Application letter, CSC Personal Data Sheet (Form 212), and supporting documents.

3. Initial screening

HR reviews submissions against the qualification standards for the position.

4. Panel interview

Shortlisted applicants are interviewed by the selection/promotion board.

General qualification standards

Specific requirements vary by position, but generally follow CSC qualification standards: relevant education, appropriate CSC or professional eligibility, and relevant work experience.
