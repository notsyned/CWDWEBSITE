Services

From a brand-new connection to reporting a leak, here's what CAMIWD handles and what you'll need for each.

New Service Connection

Steps to apply for a new residential or commercial water connection.

1. Submit requirements

Application form, valid ID, proof of property/lot ownership or authorization.

2. Site inspection

CAMIWD staff inspect the connection site and assess feasibility.

3. Pay connection fee

Settle the applicable connection and meter installation fee.

4. Installation

Meter installed and service activated, typically within [processing time — confirm].

Other services

Reconnection

Restore service after disconnection due to non-payment, once dues are settled.

Meter testing

Request a meter accuracy test if you believe your reading is incorrect.

Leak reporting

Report visible leaks in mainlines or service lines for repair scheduling.

Transfer of ownership

Update account records when a property or connection changes hands.

Change of meter location

Request relocation of an existing meter within the same property.

Senior citizen / PWD discount

Applicable billing discounts per RA 9994 and RA 10754, subject to verification.
