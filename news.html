CSC Compressed Workweek Guidelines Now Cover All Government Agencies and GOCCs

The Civil Service Commission (CSC) has amended its Flexible Work Arrangement (FWA) policy to give government agencies and GOCCs — including the Camiling Water District — greater flexibility in adopting a compressed workweek (CWW).

Under the updated guidelines, employees may complete the mandatory 40-hour workweek within four days instead of the usual five. Agencies determine their own daily work hours and designate the additional day off, provided public services remain fully available Monday through Friday — including for frontline and on-site staff, subject to the agency head's approval.

The policy stems from OP Memorandum Circular No. 114 (March 6, 2026), which directed a four-day onsite work arrangement across government as an energy-conservation measure. Key points:

  • No reduction in benefits — the standard 1.25 days each of vacation and sick leave continue to accrue monthly.
  • Public-facing services must remain accessible all five working days regardless of individual staff schedules.
  • Adoption applies retroactively from March 6, 2026, in line with the Office of the President directive.

CAMIWD Office Hours: Monday–Thursday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (per CSC Resolution No. 2200209).

Source: Civil Service Commission press release, July 16, 2026 — csc.gov.ph
