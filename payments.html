Payment Channels

Pay your water bill through any of the channels below, or in person at the CAMIWD office.

GCash

Search "Camiling Water District" under Bills Pay in the GCash app.

Maya

Pay Bills > Government > Camiling Water District.

Link.BizPortal

Search CAMIWD under Water Utilities.

Coins.ph

Bills > Water > Camiling Water District.

AllBank

Over-the-counter bills payment at any AllBank branch.

Bayad Center

Present your account number at any Bayad Center outlet.

7-Eleven

Pay via CLiQQ kiosk or at the counter.

Palawan Pawnshop

Bills payment counter, any branch.

AllEasy

Bills payment app — search Camiling Water District.

PrimeWater App

Pay directly if your account is linked in the app.

Walk-in

Pay in person at the CAMIWD office, cash or check.

Before you pay: have your account number ready (found on your most recent billing statement). Keep your payment reference number until the payment reflects on your next bill.
